The four weekly examples (Monday to Thursday) include photos. The Monday image illustrates a medical appointment, not a claim that Med Deg provides medical treatment. Tuesday uses a companionship illustration; no supplied image depicts baking or shopping.

Presentation only: the two Olly photos use CSS sepia(0.035) and saturation(0.96); the woodland photo uses brightness(1.06) and saturation(0.96). Other photos are unfiltered. Crops use object-fit/object-position; faces are not retouched. The hero quote sits below the faces with a small overlap at the image edge. Next Image supplies optimized responsive derivatives and lazy loading for secondary images.

The hands image is 700 x 700 and limited to 340 CSS pixels, suitable for approximately 2x density at that placement. A larger original would help on 3x displays. All other selected originals exceed 4000 pixels on their long edge. No source was upscaled or permanently color-adjusted.
